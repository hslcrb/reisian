# Community conduct / 커뮤니티 행동 원칙

Reisian welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience. Participation in issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions requires respectful, constructive behavior.

## What we expect

- Discuss the work with specific examples and actionable suggestions.
- Respect names, pronouns, language differences and accessibility needs.
- Ask before sharing someone else's personal or private information.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and explain technical disagreements without personal attacks.
- Credit original work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory remarks, doxxing, deliberate disruption and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are unacceptable.

## Reporting and response

For a non-sensitive moderation concern, ask `@hslcrb` for help in the affected discussion. If a report contains personal or sensitive information, request a private contact route without posting those details. A dedicated confidential mailbox has not been established; do not assume public GitHub posts are private. Use GitHub's own reporting tools when appropriate.

Maintainers may ask for an edit, remove content, lock a discussion, or restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. They should explain the decision to the affected person when practical, protect report details, and recuse themselves from decisions in which they are directly involved. A person may request reconsideration with new context through the same contact route. No response time is guaranteed.
